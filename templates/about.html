About

What is FileDrop?

FileDrop is a no-login, no-account file and text transfer web application. It lets you move content from one device to another — a laptop to a phone, a phone to a tablet — using a temporary 7-digit code, with no intermediary apps, no cloud storage accounts, and no lasting trace on our servers.

It was built by a developer in India as an open, lightweight alternative to "WhatsApp yourself a file" workflows. FileDrop does not retain, index, analyse, or monetise any user content.

FileDrop operates as an intermediary — a conduit only. We initiate no transfers, modify no content, and select no recipients. The sender and receiver are always humans making deliberate choices.

Privacy at a Glance

Exactly what FileDrop collects — and what it never does

No guesswork. No buried clauses. Here is the full picture.

What we collect

Uploader IP address

Stored with transfer metadata. Disclosed only under a valid court order. Never sold or shared.

Upload timestamp

Used to calculate the 15-minute expiry. Deleted with the transfer.

File metadata

Filename, MIME type, and size — required to serve the file. Deleted at transfer expiry.

Standard server request logs

Generated automatically by Railway (our host). Retained per Railway's logging policy. We do not control or access these beyond debugging.

What we never collect

User accounts or identity

No registration. No names. No emails. Ever.

Tracking cookies or sessions

FileDrop sets no cookies and runs no analytics scripts.

File contents beyond the transfer window

Files are deleted from our servers the moment they are downloaded, or after 15 minutes — whichever comes first.

Third-party analytics or advertising

No Google Analytics, no Facebook Pixel, no ad SDKs of any kind.

Device fingerprints or browser data

No fingerprinting, no User-Agent profiling, no canvas tracking.

Payment or financial information

FileDrop is currently free. No billing data is collected.

Data Retention

Everything expires. Nothing lingers.

FileDrop enforces a hard 15-minute data retention window for all transfers. This is not a policy setting — it is implemented at the infrastructure level and cannot be overridden by anyone, including us.

  • Burn after download — files are permanently deleted from our servers the moment they are downloaded. The retrieval code becomes invalid immediately.
  • Auto-delete at 15 minutes — if a transfer is not retrieved within 15 minutes of creation, it is permanently deleted by an automated background process, regardless of download status.
  • Text burns on read — text transfers are deleted from our database the instant the recipient retrieves them. Refreshing the page after retrieval shows nothing.
  • No backup copies — FileDrop does not maintain backups of user-uploaded content. Once deleted, recovery is technically impossible.
Important for users Because FileDrop deletes files immediately after download and never creates backups, we cannot recover a file after deletion under any circumstances — including legal requests that arrive after the transfer window has closed.

Indian Law

Legal framework we operate under

FileDrop is built and operated in India, and is primarily governed by Indian technology and data protection laws. We have designed the platform to comply with the following statutes.

IT Act 2000 · Section 79

Safe Harbour for Intermediaries

FileDrop qualifies as an intermediary under Section 79 of the Information Technology Act, 2000. As an intermediary, we are not liable for third-party content transmitted through our platform, provided we: (a) do not initiate the transmission, (b) do not select the receiver, (c) do not modify the content, and (d) act expeditiously upon receiving actual knowledge of unlawful content. FileDrop satisfies all four conditions by design.

IT Rules 2021 · Intermediary Guidelines

Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021

FileDrop publishes this Terms of Service and Privacy Policy as required by Rule 3(1)(a). We respond to abuse reports and government orders as required by Rule 3(1)(b) and Rule 3(2). FileDrop is not classified as a "significant social media intermediary" (which requires 5 million+ registered users) and is therefore not subject to the additional obligations under Rule 4.

DPDP Act 2023

Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023

FileDrop collects minimal personal data — only the uploader's IP address and upload timestamp, both of which are deleted alongside the transfer within 15 minutes. We process this data only for the purpose of enabling the transfer and for law enforcement compliance, consistent with the principles of purpose limitation and storage limitation under the DPDP Act.

IT Act 2000 · Sections 43, 66, 66B, 66C, 67, 67A, 67B

Cybercrime Provisions

FileDrop expressly prohibits use of the platform for any activity that constitutes an offence under these sections, including unauthorised access to computer systems, data theft, identity fraud, transmission of obscene material, and distribution of child sexual abuse material. Violations are reported to CERT-In and relevant law enforcement agencies.

Copyright Act, 1957

Intellectual Property Protection

Users may not use FileDrop to distribute copyrighted works without the rights holder's authorisation. FileDrop will respond to valid copyright infringement notices submitted to our abuse contact within 36 hours. Given our 15-minute retention window, most transfers will have self-deleted before a notice can be acted upon.

Global

International users

FileDrop is accessible globally. While Indian law is our primary legal framework, we acknowledge the following international standards and aim to align with their principles.

GDPR — European Union

General Data Protection Regulation

FileDrop's data minimisation approach aligns with GDPR principles. We collect only what is technically necessary (IP address, timestamp), delete data within 15 minutes, and do not share data with third parties for commercial purposes. EU users should be aware that FileDrop is operated from India, and data is processed on servers hosted by Railway (US) and Upstash (US-based infrastructure with Indian region options).

DMCA — United States

Digital Millennium Copyright Act

FileDrop honours DMCA takedown notices submitted to our abuse contact. Given our 15-minute retention window, infringing content will typically have been automatically deleted before a notice is acted upon. To submit a DMCA notice, email us with: (a) identification of the copyrighted work, (b) the transfer code if known, (c) your contact details, and (d) a statement of good faith belief. We respond within 48 hours.
